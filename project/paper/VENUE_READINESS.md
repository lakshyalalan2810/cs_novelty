## Recommended target

**Recommend IEEE Access, provisionally as a Research Article.** Its stated simulation category and explicit room for negative results best match this package's combination of narrow supported entry discrimination, unfavorable/null outcomes, and auditable failure-driven development. The stronger diagnosis/stability literature should be discussed openly; venue breadth does not make those missing guarantees available. Use the Research Article category because this manuscript has a positive confirmatory finding as well as negative branches; do not automatically relabel the whole study as a Negative Result article. [Submission categories](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/), [technical acceptance criteria](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/preparing-your-article/).

The official APC is currently **US$2,160 plus applicable local taxes**. Authors must decide whether funding is available; no payment or waiver eligibility is assumed. If the APC cannot be funded, Transactions of the Institute of Measurement and Control is the sensible alternative to consider: its current guide lists no mandatory submission/publication fee on the subscription route, with optional paid open access. [IEEE Access APC](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/about/article-processing-charges/), [Sage guide](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/tim).

This recommendation does not require new experiments. The appropriate next work is archive release, exact reporting, formatting, and human metadata. If reviewers require hardware, broader operating conditions, or new theoretical guarantees, that would be a separate scientific project; they cannot be supplied through editorial changes to this frozen package.

## Required target-specific actions before submission

For IEEE Access, the current checklist requires a corresponding author, a populated publicly visible ORCID for the submitting author, biographies for all authors, and 3–10 keywords. AI-generated text must be acknowledged and affected sections must cite the system used. Human authors must review those disclosures and the final text. Relevant references must be checked for accuracy and retraction status. [Official checklist](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/).

These facts must be supplied or confirmed by the authors, not guessed:

- Corresponding/submitting author choice, active email, author order, exact affiliations, and ORCID.
- Verified short biographies, acknowledgments, funding/grants or absence of funding, and conflicts or absence of conflicts.
- Author contribution assignments where needed by the chosen venue; coauthor approval of submission.
- Accurate data/code/supplement availability with real public links and an archive DOI only after deposit; licenses and rights to distribute artifacts.
- Accurate AI assistance disclosure based on actual use, and any venue-required ethics/declaration applicability. No ethics approval, exemption, or author statement is invented here.
- APC funding decision and final target/article-type decision.

The generic IEEE conference source is **not** the required IEEE Access submission template. After the target decision, convert the class/front matter, add required author material, then repeat the real compiler/citation checks and page-by-page visual QA. The present task's final PDF remains a corrected review draft rather than an Access-compliant submission package.

For JCAES, the inspected instructions require corresponding-author email and submission-system author contributions/competing interests, plus relevant declarations and data availability. For TIMC, they require funding/conflict/ethics-applicability headings and encourage a data availability statement. Do not use boilerplate “none” or “not applicable” until the authors verify the facts. [JCAES instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/40313/submission-guidelines), [TIMC instructions](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/tim).
